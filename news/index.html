Announcement May 2026

Au is now available on the App Store

After months of building, testing, and refining — Au is live. You can download it today, connect your bank account in under two minutes, and see the true cost of every purchase before you make it. We built Au because every other finance app tells you what you did wrong after the money is already gone. Au shows you the consequence before you spend. That's the only moment that actually changes behaviour.

Product April 2026

Why we measure everything in days, not dollars

$310 means nothing. $310 is an abstraction. But "26 days further from your house deposit" — that means something. It connects to your life. The decision to express every financial consequence in time rather than money is the single most important design choice in Au, and it took us a long time to get there. Thinking March 2026

The problem with every finance app (and why we built Au)

Seventy percent of people who download a personal finance app delete it within thirty days. The reason isn't apathy — it's shame. Every app is built around the same broken model: show people what they did wrong, make them feel bad, hope that guilt turns into better behaviour. It doesn't. Au is built on a completely different idea.

Feature February 2026

Introducing Atom — your AI financial advisor

Atom is Au's AI, powered by Claude. It knows everything about your financial life and you can talk to it about any of it — text or voice, any time. Serious questions like "what happens to my goals if I take a pay cut?" and playful ones like "what if I bought a Batmobile?" Both get real, data-backed answers drawn from your actual situation.
