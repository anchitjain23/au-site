About Au

Built for the moment
before you spend.

Au is a forward-looking financial intelligence platform — the first product built to operate at the exact moment a financial decision is being made, not after it.

The name

Au takes its name from gold — chemical symbol Au, atomic number 79, known in Latin as Aurum. One of the oldest, most universally recognised symbols of value on earth. Rare. Enduring. Precise. That is exactly what Au the product is built to be.

The periodic table is the DNA of the brand. The Au element — symbol, atomic number, name — appears throughout the product. It is not decoration. It is a statement about what Au stands for.

The problem we're solving

Millions of people download a finance app every month. Seventy percent delete it within thirty days. Not because they don't care about money. Because the app made them feel worse about it.

Every major finance app — Mint, YNAB, Cleo, Monarch — is built around the same broken idea: track what you spent, show you the damage, make you feel guilty. They are forensic tools for a crime that already happened. By the time the app tells you that you overspent, the money is gone. The moment has passed. The information is useless.

The most powerful moment in personal finance is not the review — it is the decision. The second before you buy something. That is when the right information changes behaviour. That is the moment every app misses. Au is the only product built to operate at that exact moment.

Our principles

No red. No shame. Consequences are shown as facts — "+8 days" — not judgments. The user decides what to do with the information.

Days, not dollars. Every purchase is expressed in time against your goals. 10 means nothing. 26 days further from your house deposit means everything.

Forward, not backward. Au does not track your past. It shapes your future. Intelligence at the moment it changes behaviour.

Every number connects. Change a goal allocation and every wishlist item updates instantly. The whole picture, always honest.

The team

Au is built by a small, focused team with deep roots in product and engineering. We are bootstrapped — no external investors. Every decision is made with our users' interests first.

We are based in Toronto, Canada, and incorporated as Aurum Technologies Inc.
