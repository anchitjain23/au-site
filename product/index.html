The Product

Intelligence at the
moment it matters.

Every feature in Au is built around one idea: the most important moment in personal finance is the second before you spend, not the month after.

Anchor Features

The foundation — features every Au user uses every day

🏦

Bank Account Sync

Connects to 12,000+ financial institutions via Plaid. Every transaction pulled in automatically and reflected in your goals within minutes. Read-only — Au never touches your money.

Example: Pay rent on the 1st, coffee on the 3rd. By the 6th, both are categorised. You did nothing.
🤖

AI Transaction Categorisation

Every transaction sorted automatically. It handles the messy ones — "SQ*BLUEBOTTLE" becomes Coffee. The AI learns from corrections. 95%+ accuracy within 60 days.

Example: Three DoorDash orders correctly filed as Food Delivery — not just Food & Drink — so insights stay specific.
📊

Real-Time Dashboard

Net worth, income vs spend, top categories, and goal progress — all live. Not end-of-day. When you spend $12 on lunch, the dashboard reflects it within minutes.

Example: It's the 20th. You've spent $2,400 of your usual $3,200 budget. Your house deposit is 37% complete. All in one glance.
🎯

Goal Setting & Tracking

Any number of goals running simultaneously. Au calculates the realistic path based on your actual income and spending — not a generic calculator. Completion dates update automatically as your habits change.

Example: You cut takeout by $200/month. Your house deposit moves forward from October 2027 to August 2027.
💡

Au Insights Engine

Specific, goal-linked, actionable intelligence. Not generic warnings — patterns you don't see yourself, quantified in time, surfaced before you need them.

Example: "You've used Uber 14 times this month. Cutting back to your usual saves $180 and moves your Japan holiday 12 days closer."

Primary Features

What makes Au unlike anything else in the market

⚡

Goal Impact Bar

Au's flagship. Every wishlist item shows how many days it pushes your goal back. When you skip an item, Au celebrates: "You just saved 8 days. Your house deposit is closer."

Example: $549 Vitamix blender → your Bali holiday moves back 18 days. You skip it.
🛍

AI Personal Shopper

Add any item to your wishlist and Au evaluates it completely — not just whether you can afford it, but whether it's the right time given your goals, bills, and savings rate. Import directly from Amazon or screenshot any wishlist.

Example: Screenshot 7 ASOS items. Au imports all 7 and flags the two that only push your holiday back 3 days combined.
🔍

Dupe Finder

Automatically finds high-quality cheaper alternatives for anything in your wishlist. The Goal Impact Bar updates in real time as you toggle between original and dupe. Au earns an affiliate commission — you pay nothing extra.

Example: Dyson Airwrap ($599) → BaByliss Air Style ($89, 4.7 stars). Save $510 and gain 17 days toward your goal.
⚛️

Atom — Your AI

Powered by Claude. Knows your complete financial life. Text or voice, any time. Serious questions and wild simulations — all backed by your real data.

Example: "Should I buy this $1,200 laptop?" → "Buying now pushes your Japan trip back 5 weeks. Wait until your bonus and it drops to 2 weeks."
🌙

Mood Spending Detection

Identifies when you're most likely making impulse decisions. When you add something during a high-risk window, Au adds one quiet line of context. Never a block. Never a lecture. Just an honest data point.

Example: 11:47pm Friday. You add a $280 jacket. Au: "This is your highest-spend time of the week. Historically you remove 60% of things added at this hour."
🌟

Spending Personality Profile

After 30 days, Au generates a personalised profile of how you actually relate to money. Shareable — designed to be posted, screenshotted, sent. Drives organic word-of-mouth.

Example: "The Experience Collector. You spend intentionally on travel and food. Your blind spot is subscription creep — 7 active subs unused this month."
🔮

Future You Simulator

Toggle purchases on and off and watch your projected financial future update in real time. Two versions of your future side by side — the gap between them is the most motivating number in personal finance.

Example: Cancel two unused subs and skip three wishlist items. Future You at 35 goes from $4,200 saved to $11,800.
🏠

Au Together

Shared household goals, combined income tracking, per-member spend views, and joint insights. Works for couples, roommates, families, anyone. Each person keeps their own private view.

Example: Combined income $9,400, combined spend $6,200, shared deposit goal 44% complete — one dashboard.
💸

Bill Negotiation Coach

Identifies every bill where you're likely overpaying and generates a personalised negotiation script from Atom — based on your specific provider, tenure, and current market rates. Included in Au Atomic. Always.

Example: Internet bill at $89/mo. Average in your area: $61. Atom writes your script. You call and get it to $65. $288 saved this year.
