Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Au account and all associated data at any time.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Au app and go to Profile
  2. Select Delete Account
  3. Confirm by typing DELETE

Your account will be permanently deleted immediately — there is no waiting period, and this action cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently erase your profile, financial goals, transactions (bank-synced and manually entered), budgets, wishlist items, AI chat history and insights, spending categorization rules, generated financial reports, notification settings, and all authentication records. We also revoke access to any linked bank connections through Plaid.

No account data is retained after deletion.

Questions about account deletion?

Contact us at au@au79.app and we'll assist you.

Don't have the app installed? Email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.
