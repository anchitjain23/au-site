Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · Aurum Technologies Inc.

1. Information We Collect

Au collects information you provide directly — such as your email address when you create an account — and financial data retrieved through Plaid, our banking data partner. We do not store your bank login credentials. All financial data is accessed in read-only mode.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to operate the Au service, provide personalised financial insights, calculate Goal Impact scores, and improve the accuracy of our AI categorisation. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Sharing

We share data only with service providers required to operate Au — including Plaid (banking connections), Anthropic (AI), and cloud infrastructure providers. All partners are bound by data processing agreements. We do not share data for advertising purposes.

4. Advertising

Au Oxygen (free tier) displays non-targeted contextual advertisements — relevant to what is on screen, never based on user data or browsing history. Paid subscribers (Au Atomic and Au Fusion) are completely ad-free.

5. Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. We follow industry-standard practices for access control and data protection. Plaid connections use bank-level encryption.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at anchitj@au79.app. You may also disconnect your bank accounts and delete your Au account at any time from within the app.

7. Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove your personal information within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain it.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests: anchitj@au79.app
