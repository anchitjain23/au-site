Features

Everything Au
can do for you.

Three sets of features. One idea — the most important moment in your financial life is right now, before you decide. Not after.

01 · Atom

The AI that knows
your entire financial life.

01 — Atom AI

Your AI that knows
your entire financial life.

Atom isn't a chatbot. It knows your goals, your spending, your income, your net worth — and you can ask it anything, anywhere in the app. Spot something on your Spend screen? Ask Atom. Looking at a goal? Ask Atom.

It's woven into every screen, ready the second you need it.

Example: "Should I buy this $1,200 laptop?" → "Buying now pushes Japan back 5 weeks. Wait for your bonus and it drops to 2 weeks."

02 — Insights Engine

Already watching.
Before you even ask.

Atom doesn't wait for your questions. It's constantly scanning your patterns, spotting things you'd never catch yourself, and surfacing them before they cost you.

Not generic warnings — specific, goal-linked intelligence that tells you exactly what a habit is costing you in time.

Example: "14 Uber rides this month — $180 above usual. Cutting back moves your Japan holiday 12 days closer."

03 — Atom Playground

Build your own
financial tools.

No other finance app gives you this. Atom Playground lets you create custom dashboards, one-tap macros, and automated watch rules — all connected to your live financial data.

Tell Atom what you want to build in plain language. It handles everything else.

Example: "Every Friday, show me this week's spend and how it moved my goals." Atom builds it, saves it, runs it with one tap — forever.

04 — Dream Path

Your biggest goals.
With a real plan.

Set a goal and Au doesn't just track it — it hands you the exact steps to reach it. What to adjust, what to cut, what to prioritise, in what order.

Based on your actual numbers, not generic advice. Not a projection. A path.

Example: House deposit in 14 months. Dream Path shows: increase goal allocation by $180/mo, cut two unused subscriptions, skip the three highest-impact wishlist items. Date moves to 11 months.

02 · Before You Buy

The only app working
at the moment that actually matters.

01 — Shopping Assistant

Everything you want.
What it actually costs you.

Add anything to your wishlist — from any store, any retailer. Au shows you exactly what it costs toward your goals and finds you a Scoop — a high-quality, cheaper alternative with its own impact already calculated.

One screen. Instant clarity before you spend a thing.

Example: Sony headphones at $349 = +8 days. Au's Scoop: BaByliss alternative at $89 = +3 days. Save $260, gain 5 days toward your goal.

02 — Goals

Every goal, live.
Every decision, connected.

Set as many goals as you want — house deposit, holiday, emergency fund, anything. Au calculates the exact path to each one based on your real income and spending.

Adjust your monthly allocation and every projected date updates instantly. Every purchase you consider shows how many days it costs you toward the things you're actually working for.

Example: Cut takeout by $200/month and your house deposit date pulls forward from October 2027 to August 2027.

03 — Mood Spending Detection

Au knows when
you're about to slip.

Late nights. Post-payday. Sunday evenings. Au learns your highest-risk spending windows and adds one quiet line of context when you're in one.

It never blocks you. It never lectures. Just the right information at exactly the right moment.

Example: 11:47pm Friday. You add a $280 jacket. Au adds quietly: "This is your highest-spend window. You remove 60% of things added at this hour."

04 — Bill Negotiation

You're overpaying
on bills. Au fixes that.

Au identifies every bill where you're paying above market rate — internet, insurance, phone, streaming. Then Atom writes you the exact script to negotiate it down, tailored to your provider and how long you've been a customer.

You make the call. Au tracks the saving.

Example: Internet at $89/mo. Average in your area: $61. Atom writes the script. You call. New rate: $65/mo. $288 saved this year.

03 · Your Money Life

Au doesn't just track your money.
It understands you.

01 — Spending Personality

Your money.
Your personality.

After 30 days Au knows exactly how you relate to money. Your patterns, your blind spots, your strengths — captured in a short, sharp profile that feels uncomfortably accurate.

Share it, screenshot it, send it to your friends. The Spotify Wrapped moment for your finances.

Example: "The Experience Collector — you spend on travel and moments, never on things. Blind spot: 7 unused subscriptions costing $94/mo."

02 — Monthly Review

Your month.
The whole picture.

Every month Au puts together a complete picture of where you stood — what came in, what went out, which goals moved forward, which habits shifted.

Not a spreadsheet. A clear, personal summary of your financial month that actually means something.

Example: "May — you saved $840, hit your emergency fund goal, and cut food delivery by $190 from last month. Net worth up $1,240."

03 — Future You Simulator

Two versions of you.
Same income. Different decisions.

Toggle purchases on and off and watch your financial future update in real time. One version of you keeps spending as you are. The other makes different choices.

The gap between them is the most motivating number in personal finance.

Example: Future You at 35 has $4,200 at your current pace. Toggle off 3 wishlist items and cancel 2 subscriptions — Future You has $11,800.
