Features

Intelligence at every
point of decision.

Every feature in Au is built around one idea — the most important moment in personal finance is the second before you spend, not the month after. Three ways that shows up, every day.

01 · Before You Spend

The moment that
actually matters.

01 — Goals Screen

Every goal,
always live.

Set as many goals as you want — house deposit, holiday, emergency fund. Each one shows what you've saved, what's left, and a projected date that updates automatically every time your spending changes.

The Goals page is a control panel, not a progress tracker. Adjust an allocation and every date — and every wishlist days number — updates instantly.

Example: Cut takeout by $200/month and your house deposit date pulls forward from October 2027 to August 2027.

02 — Expenses Screen

Every dollar,
goal-linked.

Every spending category shows what it's costing you against your budget — and against your goals, in days. Not just "$340 on dining" but what that $340 actually means.

Run an Atom simulation on any category and watch the Combined Goal Impact tile update live, before you commit to anything.

Example: Dining Out at $340 — $110 above your usual pace — is costing you 4 extra days on your house deposit.

03 — Shopper Screen

Buy less.
Or buy smarter.

Every wishlist item shows its days impact — and right below it, a high-quality cheaper alternative with its own impact. One screen, instant comparison.

No toggling, no extra taps. The smarter choice is just sitting there next to the original.

Example: Sony WH-1000XM5 at $349 = +8 days. The BaByliss dupe at $89 = +3 days. Save $260, gain 5 days.

02 · Meet Atom

An AI that knows your
whole financial life.

04 — Atom AI

Ask Atom
anything.

Atom is Au's AI, powered by Claude. It knows your goals, your spending, your income, your net worth — and you can talk to it about any of it, in text or voice, backed by your real data.

Serious questions and wild simulations. Both get real, personalised answers.

Example: "Should I buy this $1,200 laptop?" → "Buying now pushes Japan back 5 weeks. Wait for your bonus and it drops to 2 weeks."

05 — Future You Simulator

Two futures,
side by side.

Toggle purchases on and off in your wishlist and watch your projected financial future update in real time. One future where you keep spending as you are. One where you make the changes Atom suggests.

The gap between them is the most motivating number in personal finance.

Example: Future You at 35 has $4,200 at your current pace. Toggle off 3 wishlist items and cancel 2 subscriptions — Future You has $11,800. Same income, different decisions.

06 — Insights Engine

Patterns,
before they cost you.

Au notices things you don't — and quantifies exactly what they cost in time against your goals. Insights are proactive, not reactive. They surface before the damage is done, not after.

It never tells you off. It shows you the consequence clearly and lets you decide.

Example: "14 Uber rides this month — $180 above usual. Cutting back moves your Japan holiday 12 days closer."

03 · Made To Share

Your money,
in perspective.

07 — Spending Personality

Know your
money type.

After 30 days of data, Au generates a personalised spending personality — a short, sharp, accurate description of how you actually relate to money. Your patterns, your blind spots, your strengths.

Shareable by design — built to spread the way Spotify Wrapped does.

Example: "The Experience Collector — you spend intentionally on travel and moments, rarely on things. Blind spot: 7 unused subscriptions costing $94/mo."

08 — Dream Big

Your goals,
in perspective.

Pick any celebrity from Au's curated list. Au breaks their net worth into the assets people actually know — the houses, the jets, the watch collections — and calculates how long it would take you to get there at your current pace.

It's absurd on purpose. It makes your real goals feel achievable by comparison.

Example: Leonardo DiCaprio's Malibu compound: $10.7M — 89 years at your pace. "You won't be Leo in this lifetime. But your Bali holiday is 6 months away."

09 — Au Together

Shared goals,
shared wins.

Combined income tracking, per-member spend views, and joint Au Insights. Each person keeps their own private view, but everyone can see the shared household picture when they choose to.

Works for couples, roommates, families — any combination of people.

Example: Combined income $9,400, combined spend $6,200, house deposit at 44%. "Skip dining out twice a week and your deposit date moves from March 2028 to November 2027."
