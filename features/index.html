Features

Intelligence at every
point of decision.

Every feature in Au is built around one idea — the most important moment in personal finance is the second before you spend, not the month after.

01 — Dashboard

Your wealth,
live.

Net worth, income, spending, and goal progress — all live. When you spend $12 on lunch, the dashboard reflects it within minutes. Every number connected to everything else.

Connects to 12,000+ banks via Plaid. Read-only. Bank-level security. Setup in under two minutes.

Example: It's the 20th. You've spent $2,400 of your usual $3,200 budget. House deposit at 62%. Emergency fund at 81%. All in one glance.

02 — Goal Impact Bar

Days, not
dollars.

Every wishlist item shows one number: how many days it pushes your goal back. Not the price. Not a percentage. Days. The only reframe that makes a purchase feel real before you make it.

When you skip an item Au celebrates. When you buy it, Au shows the honest impact — no guilt, no lecture.

Example: That $349 pair of headphones isn't $349. It's 8 days further from your house deposit.

03 — Atom AI

Your AI
knows everything.

Atom is Au's AI, powered by Claude. It knows your goals, your spending, your income, your net worth — and you can talk to it about any of it, in text or voice, backed by your real data.

Serious questions and wild simulations. Both get real, personalised answers.

Example: "Should I buy this $1,200 laptop?" → "Buying now pushes Japan back 5 weeks. Wait for your bonus and it drops to 2 weeks."

04 — Spend Page

Every habit,
goal-linked.

Every spending category shows exactly what it's costing you in days toward your goals. Not just dollars. See what your patterns actually mean in terms of the things you're working toward.

Run Atom simulations on any category — watch your goal dates update live before committing to anything.

Example: Dining Out at $340 this month — $110 above your usual pace — is costing you 4 extra days on your house deposit.
