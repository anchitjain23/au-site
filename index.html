Core Features

Built for the moment
before you spend

⚡

Goal Impact Bar

Every item in your wishlist shows one number: how many days it delays your goal. Not the price. Days. The single reframe that makes every purchase decision immediate and personal.

Example: A $549 Vitamix moves your Bali holiday back 18 days. You skip it. Au responds: "Bali is 18 days closer."
🏦

Bank Sync

Connects to 12,000+ financial institutions via Plaid. Every transaction pulled in automatically, categorised, and reflected in your goals within minutes. Read-only — Au never touches your money.

Example: You pay rent on the 1st, grab coffee on the 3rd. By the 6th, both are already categorised. You did nothing.
🎯

Goal Tracking

Set any number of goals — house deposit, holiday, emergency fund. Au calculates the realistic path based on your actual income and spending, and updates your completion date every time your habits change.

Example: You cut takeout by $200/month. Your house deposit date moves forward 2 months. Automatically.
⚛️

Atom — Your AI

Powered by Claude. Atom knows your complete financial life and answers any question — text or voice. Serious analysis or wild simulations, all backed by your real data.

Example: "Should I buy this $1,200 laptop?" → "Buying now pushes your Japan trip back 5 weeks. Wait until your bonus and it drops to 2 weeks."
🔍

Dupe Finder

Add anything to your wishlist and Au finds high-quality cheaper alternatives. See exactly how much faster the dupe gets you to your goal. Au earns a commission from the retailer — you pay nothing extra.

Example: Dyson Airwrap at $599 vs BaByliss at $89 — 4.7 stars, 12K reviews. You save $510 and gain 17 days toward your goal.
💡

Au Insights Engine

Proactive, goal-linked intelligence that surfaces before you need it. Specific patterns connected directly to your timeline in days, not dollars.

Example: "You've spent $310 on Uber this month — $180 above your usual. Cutting back moves your Japan holiday 12 days closer."

Impact Bar

When you skip an item, Au celebrates the days you saved. When you buy it, Au shows the honest impact — no guilt, no lecture.
