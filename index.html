A new way to see money

That $349 pair of headphones
isn't $349.
It's 8 days further
from your house deposit.

Au converts every purchase into time against the goals you actually care about. That single reframe changes everything.

01 — Dashboard

Your wealth,
live.

Net worth, income, spending, and goal progress — all updating in real time. When you spend $12 on lunch, the dashboard reflects it within minutes. Every number connected, nothing isolated.

Connect to 12,000+ banks and credit unions via Plaid. Read-only. Bank-level security. Setup in under two minutes.

Example: It's the 20th. You've spent $2,400 of your usual $3,200 budget. House deposit at 62%. Emergency fund at 81%. All in one glance, in five seconds.

02 — Goal Impact Bar

Days, not
dollars.

Every item in your wishlist shows one number: how many days it pushes your goal back. Not the price. Not a percentage. Days. The only reframe that makes a purchase feel real before you make it.

When you skip an item, Au celebrates. When you buy it, Au shows the honest impact with no guilt, no lecture. The decision is always yours.

Example: Dyson Airwrap at $599 pushes your house deposit back 20 days. The dupe — $89, 4.7 stars — pushes it back 3. You save $510 and gain 17 days.

03 — Atom AI

Your AI
knows everything.

Atom is Au's AI, powered by Claude. It knows your goals, your spending, your income, your net worth — and answers anything in text or voice, backed entirely by your real data.

Serious questions about whether to take a pay cut. Wild simulations about buying a Batmobile. Both answered with the same precision.

Example: "Should I buy this $1,200 laptop?" → "Your emergency fund is at 60%. Buying now pushes Japan back 5 weeks. Wait for your bonus and it drops to 2 weeks."

04 — Spend Page

Every habit,
goal-linked.

Every spending category shows exactly what it's costing you in days toward your goals — not just dollar amounts. See what your patterns actually mean in terms of the things you're working toward.

Lock categories to protect them from Atom's simulations. Run instant scenarios on any category and watch your goal dates update in real time before committing.

Example: Dining Out at $340 this month — $110 above your usual pace — is costing you 4 extra days on your house deposit. Atom shows you exactly which three meals did it.

05 — Spending Personality

Know how you
really spend.

After 30 days, Au generates a personalised spending personality profile — a sharp, accurate read of how you actually relate to money. Your patterns, your blind spots, your strengths.

Designed to be screenshotted, shared, sent. Drives organic word-of-mouth the way Spotify Wrapped does — every share is someone discovering Au.

Example: "The Experience Collector. You spend intentionally on travel and food. Your blind spot: 7 active subscriptions unused this month, quietly costing you $94."
