Common questions

Is Au really free?

Yes. Au Oxygen is free forever with no credit card required. You get one bank account, one goal, the Goal Impact Bar, basic Atom, and 3 Dupe Finder uses per month.

How does Au connect to my bank?

Au uses Plaid — the same network used by Venmo and Coinbase. The connection is read-only. Au can see your transactions but cannot move your money. Your bank credentials are never stored by Au.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from within the app or your App Store settings. If you're on an annual plan, you retain access until the end of your billing period.

How does Dupe Finder make money?

When you tap through to a recommended alternative, Au earns an affiliate commission from the retailer (typically 3–8%). You pay exactly the same price as going direct.

What is Atom?

Atom is Au's AI, powered by Claude. It knows your complete financial picture and answers anything in text or voice. Basic Atom is free; full access is in Au Atomic and Fusion.
