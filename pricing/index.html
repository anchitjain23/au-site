Common questions

Is Au really free?

Yes. Au Oxygen is free forever with no credit card required. You get one bank account, one goal, the Goal Impact Bar, basic Atom AI, and 3 Dupe Finder uses per month.

How does Au connect to my bank?

Au uses Plaid — the same financial data network used by Venmo, Coinbase, and thousands of other apps. The connection is read-only. Au can see your transactions but cannot move your money. Your bank credentials are never stored by Au.

What is the Goal Impact Bar?

The Goal Impact Bar shows you how many days any purchase delays your financial goals — before you buy it. A $349 purchase that pushes your house deposit back 8 days feels different than just $349. That reframe is the core of Au.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from within the app or your App Store settings. If you're on an annual plan, you'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

What is Atom?

Atom is Au's AI, powered by Claude. It knows your complete financial picture — goals, spending, income, net worth — and you can talk to it in text or voice. Basic Atom is included in the free tier; full text and voice access is available in Au Atomic and Fusion.

How does Au make money on Dupe Finder?

When you tap through to a recommended alternative, Au earns an affiliate commission from the retailer (typically 3–8%). You pay exactly the same price as going direct. Au earns nothing from the original item — only when you find something cheaper.
